Environmental Advisory and Compliance

Waste, permits, emissions, energy: behind every environmental obligation there is data that, read well, shows where the company wastes and where it can save. If it stays in separate offices, the company pays twice and misses the chance to use it.

The number1.3 Mtoe of energy saved in ten years through 40,000 energy audits, equal to the consumption of more than 4 million households (ENEA).

From compliance to value

Waste register and RENTRI, environmental permits, ISO 14001 management systems, emissions inventory, energy efficiency: every obligation produces data. My job is to make sure that data is right once and serves everything: compliance, the sustainability report, the client questionnaire, the bank.

Then comes the part the board and the bank care about, and the one that saves money: the financial analysis and quantification of environmental and climate impacts and risks, and the choices that reduce them, from energy efficiency to renewables and energy management, which cut costs and risk at the same time.

Efficiency is not theory: in ten years of mandatory energy audits, according to ENEA, over 40,000 audits led to more than 17,000 measures and savings of over 1.3 million tonnes of oil equivalent, the consumption of more than 4 million households.

How I work

I coordinate technical and environmental specialists and lead the project: from mapping obligations to measuring emissions, through to the decarbonisation plan and the return on investment. At KPMG I was deputy coordinator of the GHG Champions, the internal team of specialists on emissions KPIs and climate impacts.

Who it is for

Manufacturing and service companies that want a single counterpart across environmental compliance and sustainability, multi-site businesses, companies that want a credible decarbonisation plan that cuts costs and convinces clients and lenders.
