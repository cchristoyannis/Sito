ESG Training for Companies and Professionals

Teaching forces me to make complex things simple. It is the best way to take data out of the office and turn it into mindset and corporate culture, in companies as among professionals.

The number+0.6% value added per worker associated with each extra percentage point of trained workers (CEP, London School of Economics).

Corporate ESG training

Programmes for boards, management and the functions that produce the data: finance, procurement, operations, human resources. Goal: understand what rules, banks and clients ask for, but above all how to use that data to decide: where energy is wasted, where the supply chain is fragile, what improves the rating and the cost of credit. Data collection moves from a duty to a management tool.

Training is an investment that pays back: according to a study by the London School of Economics' Centre for Economic Performance, raising the share of trained workers by one point is associated with about 0.6% higher value added per worker.

Professional ESG training

For chartered accountants, auditors and consultants: sustainability reporting and assurance, VSME, ethics and independence of the assurance provider, ESG and credit. I have taught courses for statutory auditors on the MEF register, valid for training credits and for qualification, and given accredited training sessions for the Orders of Chartered Accountants of Rome and Teramo.

At university

Adjunct lecturer in Metrics of Sustainable Impact at NABA's Master in Sustainable Innovation Communication, subject expert in Corporate Reporting Systems and Non-Financial Disclosure at Unitelma Sapienza University. At UNINT I have led training days on the auditor's role, a practical and methodological discussion with students; in the same format I presented the ESG advisory case to MBA students of Luiss Business School and Imperial Business School.

In all, I have brought ESG training to three university programmes, a second-level master's, a master's degree and a bachelor's degree, and to some fifteen accredited sessions for the Orders of Chartered Accountants of Rome and Teramo.

SourcesDearden, Reed e Van Reenen, The Impact of Training on Productivity and Wages (CEP, LSE)
